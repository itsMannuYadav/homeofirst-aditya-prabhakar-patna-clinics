## Primary Types of Joint & Bone Disorders

Understanding the exact diagnosis helps select the most effective homeopathic treatment:

### 1. Osteoarthritis (Degenerative Joint Disease)
The most common form of arthritis, primarily affecting aging adults or individuals with past joint trauma. It involves wear-and-tear degradation of smooth articular cartilage protecting bone ends. As cartilage erodes, bones rub together, causing pain, stiffness, bone spurs (*osteophytes*), and limited mobility.

### 2. Rheumatoid Arthritis (Autoimmune Arthritis)
A chronic autoimmune systemic disease where the immune system attacks the *synovium* (joint lining). It presents symmetrically—affecting both wrists, hands, or knees simultaneously. It causes painful swelling, warmth, morning stiffness lasting over an hour, and progressive joint deformities if left untreated.

### 3. Gout & Hyperuricemia
A painful metabolic arthritis caused by elevated serum uric acid levels that form needle-like monosodium urate crystals in joint spaces. It presents with sudden, excruciating, throbbing pain, heat, and redness in the big toe, ankle, or knee.

### 4. Ankylosing Spondylitis
An inflammatory arthritis affecting the spine and sacroiliac joints, causing back stiffness and eventual vertebral fusion.
